From the founder

Why this exists.

Note from Damian Mathews, founder

I started Bridgeworks because the AI consulting market is bifurcated in a frustrating way. On one side: global firms charging six figures for slide decks. On the other: solo prompt engineers selling Zapier flows. Almost nothing in the middle that actually ships.

Meanwhile, the businesses that need AI most, the ones with real revenue and real workflow pain, sit waiting for someone to install the boring high-leverage things: the column-reference checker, the qualifying chatbot, the internal assistant that knows where the policy lives.

Bridgeworks is that middle. A small team that does the unglamorous AI work senior firms hand to junior consultants and solo builders skip because it doesn't demo well. We embed for ninety days, or we stay for two years. Either way, what we leave behind is software running in production, not slides.

— Damian Mathews Founder · Bridgeworks
How we work

Five principles we don't negotiate on.

These were written down after we refused work for them. Each one corresponds to a specific lesson from a specific engagement.

01

Every retainer client gets a named owner. The person who picks up the phone is on the team that builds.

02

We publish what we build, anonymized, so the next client benefits from work we already shipped.

03

We refuse engagements we wouldn't recommend a friend take. The first call is honest about fit.

04

We tell you when AI isn't the answer. Sometimes the right system is a checklist.

05

Patterns travel from public work into private work. Never the other direction.

Anti-positioning

What we don't do.

Things we get asked to do that we say no to. Posted here so the no comes earlier and the right conversations start faster.

  • We don't sell pure AI strategy decks. If we can't ship something working inside the engagement, the engagement isn't right.
  • We don't compete on price against offshore dev shops. If pricing is the primary axis, we're the wrong fit.
  • We don't sign engagements before a diagnostic. Two weeks of scoping protects everyone from a misfit build.
  • We don't take on retainers we don't have capacity to run well. Hiring follows demand, never the other way around.
  • We don't build novel research. We adapt patterns Anthropic and the open-source community have already shipped, tuned to your business.
  • We don't ghost. Slow news is still news.
