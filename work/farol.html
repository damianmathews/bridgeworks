The setup

A six-phase quarterly cycle across twenty companies.

The client, a private-equity asset manager running Funds I and II, holds twenty portfolio companies. Every quarter, the team runs a six-phase valuation cycle:

  1. Outreach: email every sponsor with the four standard asks (LTM EBITDA, mark, ownership, net debt).
  2. Receive & file: log every sponsor PDF and supplemental into the audit folder structure.
  3. Comps & precedents: refresh public peer comps and precedent M&A transactions in the workbook.
  4. Valuation: set the mark for each company in the cash flow workbook. Multiple decisions made by the team.
  5. Document updates: enter every figure into the investor report, draft the 300-word commentary per company, roll column references forward.
  6. Review & ship: internal team meeting, build consolidated report, distribute to LPs.

The friction lives in phase five. Rolling column references forward, every cell that pointed at column AU now points at AV, happens for every cell of every company every quarter. A single missed cell distorts the LP report. The commentary drafting, twenty 300-word blocks, eats analyst hours.

The build

Seven specialized agents, one per process step.

Each agent is wired to the real Anthropic API. Two agents use live web search. Six adapt patterns from Anthropic's open-source financial-services repo. The seventh, sponsor outreach, is purpose-built for the client's voice.

Production path

The road from pilot to twenty companies.

The browser artifact validates that Claude can do the cognitive work of the cycle. Production scales the integration:

  • Google Drive integration for file ingestion. Source files live in the firm's existing Drive structure.
  • Multi-company expansion. All twenty portcos get configured prompts and sector-specific comp sets.
  • Outlook integration for outreach. Drafts send from a firm mailbox after approval. Replies parsed into Phase 02.
  • Scheduled monitoring sweeps. Nightly or weekly per company, batched into a digest with severity ranking.
  • Phase pipelining. Phase 03 comps feed Phase 04. Phase 04 feeds Phase 05. Phase 06 cross-checks everything upstream.
  • Audit log and compliance export. Every prompt, output, and human approval logged for LP audit requests.
Bridgeworks does not replace the team. It replaces the column-reference-rolling, the first-pass commentary drafting, the three-way cross-check at the end of the quarter. What the team owns: the multiple decision, the LP voice, the final sign-off. Pattern that fits every finance engagement we run.
