Phase 01 · Sourcing — what we produce
Ranked target shortlists with verified context.
  • Industry and sub-sector scans aligned to your stated thesis
  • Target company profiles with size, geography, ownership, and recent activity
  • Similarity scoring 1 to 10 against your existing portfolio shape
  • Competitive context: comparable transactions, sponsor activity, market direction
  • Every row carries a verified source URL and a one-line business description
Phase 01 · Stays human
Decisions only your team can make.
  • Thesis interpretation: which signals actually fit the fund's mandate
  • Prioritization: who to pursue first, who to monitor, who to skip
  • Relationship paths: who knows whom, who to call
  • Conviction: whether the work justifies a partner meeting
Phase 02 · Diligence — what we produce
A diligence package the deal team can argue from.
  • Sponsor package intake: sponsor mark, methodology, equity bridge, cap table
  • Current financials reconciliation across covenant TTM, LTM, and quarterly bases
  • Capital structure analysis: preferred stack, accruals, residual equity to your tranche
  • Comp set construction with similarity scores and verified disclosure quality
  • Precedent transactions filtered to disclosed-multiple deals, every URL verified
  • Gap memo: what's missing from the sponsor pack and what to ask for
Phase 02 · Stays human
The deal decision.
  • Whether to invest, at what size, in what tranche
  • Deal structure: equity vs. debt, preferred terms, governance
  • How aggressively to push back on sponsor methodology
  • Co-investor and LP communication
Phase 03 · Valuation — what we produce
A six-section output package every quarter, every company.
  • Section 1: Crucial inputs. Sponsor mark, current financials, ownership, preferred stack above your tranche
  • Section 2: Sponsor vs. firm methodology. Side-by-side, implied multiples at the sponsor's numbers
  • Section 3: Comps and precedents refresh. Similarity scores, business descriptions, verified URLs
  • Section 4: Business update draft in your slide voice. 250 words. Investment Overview, Revenue, EBITDA, Liquidity, Strategic
  • Section 5: LP letter commentary. 250 to 300 words in your firm voice
  • Section 6: Open questions for the team. Organized by section. Every item flagged for human resolution
Phase 03 · Stays human
The mark and everything that informs it.
  • Multiple selection: which EV/EBITDA multiple applies to which EBITDA base
  • EBITDA basis: covenant TTM, LTM letter, or last-quarter LTM
  • Discount: same as sponsor or firm-specific
  • Treatment of post-quarter events (signed PSAs, deals closing, debt amendments)
  • Treatment of non-debt deductions (settlements, monitoring fees, legal reimbursements)
  • Ownership scope when multiple firm vehicles invest in one company
  • Final sign-off on the mark before LP materials go out
Phase 04 · Monitoring — what we produce
Continuous intelligence between quarterly cycles.
  • News, regulatory, and litigation sweeps per portfolio company
  • Executive changes at the company, sponsor, and named comps
  • Material M&A in the sector that updates the comp set
  • Sponsor activity: new funds, exits, dividend recaps
  • Weekly digest scoped to your portfolio, batched and severity-ranked
Phase 04 · Stays human
Triage and outreach.
  • Which signals are material to the mark
  • What gets escalated to the IC vs. left in the digest
  • What gets shared with LPs ahead of the next cycle
  • Who calls the sponsor and when
Phase 05 · LP reporting — what we produce
Letters, reports, and the cross-document cleanup.
  • Quarterly LP letter drafts in your firm voice
  • Consolidated quarterly investor report assembly across all portfolio companies
  • Cross-document consistency checks: investor report vs. valuation deck vs. cash flow workbook
  • Footnote verification, methodology disclosure language, and quarter-rolling references
  • Red-flag pass before the package goes to auditors or LPs
Phase 05 · Stays human
Voice, disclosure, and sign-off.
  • The firm voice. Tone, emphasis, what to lead with
  • Disclosure decisions on sensitive portfolio events
  • What to highlight to LPs ahead of the next capital call
  • Final sign-off on every number, every fact, before distribution

Working with your files

Three actions. Every cell traceable.

When the connector runs against your cash flow workbook, investor report, and valuation deck, every cell gets one of three actions. Every action is annotated with source and reasoning. Nothing is opaque.

Wrote

New content the connector produced.

Cells where the connector wrote a value or text. Every write carries a cell comment with the source file and the specific page or row the figure came from.

Example H187 LTM Adj. EBITDA $7.92M
source: Q4 letter, p.4
cross-check: covenant TTM
Confirmed

Existing values verified against source.

Cells that already had values when the file arrived. The connector cross-checks against source documents and adds a comment with the citation and any reconciliation context.

Example R19 Tranche II ownership 5.12%
confirmed against: Q4 letter
comment in cell
Flagged

Cells the connector deliberately did not fill.

Cells that require a human checkpoint. The connector annotates the cell with the open question and the candidate values it could not choose between. Mark selection lives here.

Example H188 multiple selection
candidates: sponsor 1.5x rev, firm EV/EBITDA
checkpoint: Jamal

The agents run on the Anthropic API under your contract or ours, your preference. Anthropic does not use enterprise API traffic for training. Source files stay in your Google Drive or SharePoint. We sign NDAs and data processing agreements before any sensitive material moves. Output materials are saved back to your workspace, not ours. Nothing client-specific leaves the engagement except an anonymized case study, and only with explicit permission.
We use Anthropic's native Google Drive connector or a Drive API integration scoped to specific folders. The agents read sponsor packages, financial models, and prior cycle outputs from the folders you designate. Writes happen back into the same files with cell comments showing source and reasoning. You see every action. You can revoke access at any time.
A diagnostic delivers a sample output for one portfolio company within two weeks.
