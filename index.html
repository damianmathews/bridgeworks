Phase 01 · Sourcing
Ranked target shortlists with verified context.
  • Industry and sub-sector scans aligned to the fund's stated thesis
  • Target profiles with size, geography, ownership, and recent activity
  • Similarity scoring against the existing portfolio shape
  • Every row carries a verified source URL and a one-line business description
Stays human
Thesis match. Who to pursue.
  • Thesis interpretation: which signals actually fit the mandate
  • Prioritization: who to pursue, who to monitor, who to skip
  • Relationship paths and who calls whom
Phase 02 · Diligence
A diligence package the deal team can argue from.
  • Sponsor pack intake: mark, methodology, equity bridge, cap table
  • Financials reconciliation across covenant TTM, LTM, and quarterly bases
  • Capital structure analysis: preferred stack, accruals, residual to your tranche
  • Comp set construction with similarity scores and disclosure quality
  • Precedent transactions filtered to disclosed-multiple deals only
Stays human
The deal call.
  • Whether to invest, at what size, in what tranche
  • Deal structure: equity vs debt, preferred terms, governance
  • How aggressively to push back on sponsor methodology
Phase 03 · Valuation
A six-section package, per company, per quarter.
  • Section 1: Crucial inputs (mark, financials, ownership, preferred stack)
  • Section 2: Sponsor vs firm methodology, implied multiples
  • Section 3: Comps and precedents refresh with similarity scores
  • Section 4: Business update in your slide voice (250 words)
  • Section 5: LP letter commentary (250-300 words)
  • Section 6: Open questions for the team, by section
Stays human
The mark and everything that informs it.
  • Multiple selection: which multiple, which EBITDA base
  • Discount: same as sponsor or firm-specific
  • Treatment of post-quarter events
  • Ownership scope across firm vehicles
  • Final sign-off on every external document
Phase 04 · Monitoring
Continuous intelligence between cycles.
  • News, regulatory, litigation sweeps per portfolio company
  • Executive changes at the company, sponsor, and named comps
  • Material M&A in the sector that updates the comp set
  • Weekly digest, batched and severity-ranked
Stays human
Triage and outreach.
  • Which signals are material to the mark
  • What escalates to the IC vs stays in the digest
  • What gets shared with LPs ahead of the next cycle
Phase 05 · LP reporting
Letters, reports, the cross-document cleanup.
  • Quarterly LP letter drafts in your firm voice
  • Consolidated investor report across the full portfolio
  • Cross-document consistency: investor report vs valuation deck vs cash flow
  • Footnote verification and quarter-rolling reference checks
  • Red-flag pass before the package goes to auditors or LPs
Stays human
Voice, disclosure, sign-off.
  • Firm voice. Tone, emphasis, what to lead with
  • Disclosure decisions on sensitive portfolio events
  • Final sign-off on every number, every fact, before distribution

Working with your files

Three actions.
Every cell traceable.

When the connector runs against your cash flow workbook, investor report, and valuation deck, every cell gets one of three actions. Every action is annotated with source and reasoning.

Wrote

New content the connector produced.

Cells where the connector wrote a value or text. Every write carries a cell comment with the source file and the page or row the figure came from.

Example H187 · LTM Adj. EBITDA $7.92M
source: Q4 letter, p.4
cross-check: covenant TTM
Confirmed

Existing values verified against source.

Cells already populated when the file arrived. The connector cross-checks each against the source document and adds a comment with the citation.

Example R19 · Tranche II ownership 5.12%
confirmed against: Q4 letter
comment in cell
Flagged

Cells the connector deliberately did not fill.

Cells that require a human checkpoint. The connector annotates the cell with the open question and the candidate values it could not choose between.

Example H188 · multiple selection
candidates: 1.5× rev (sponsor) or firm EV/EBITDA
checkpoint: senior associate
