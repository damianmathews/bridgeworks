# Outbound message templates

Voice: direct, operator-grade, no fluff. Match the tone of the Bridgeworks site (the wedge paragraph, the case studies). Avoid: "leverage," "synergies," "unlock," "transform," "I hope this finds you well," any sentence that could appear in 1000 other cold emails.

Personalization rule: every message must reference one specific thing about the firm or person (recent fund close, recent exit, recent hire, recent portfolio addition, recent LinkedIn post). If you can't find one, the account isn't ready — research more or drop it.

Placeholders use `{curly-braces}`. Replace before sending.

---

## Day 0 — LinkedIn connect note

300 character limit. Customize the bracketed line for every send.

```
{First name}, saw {specific signal — e.g. "you just announced Fund III", "your portfolio just added [Company]"}. Run a small AI firm that builds quarterly-cycle agents for PE shops — would value comparing notes if you're open to a quick connect.
```

**Why this works:** specific signal proves you researched. "Comparing notes" is low-stakes ask. "Small firm" signals senior craft not vendor.

## Day 2 — LinkedIn DM (after connect accepts)

```
Thanks for connecting, {First name}.

Quick context: I run Bridgeworks, a 2-person AI firm. We just shipped a 7-agent system that runs the full quarterly valuation cycle for a PE shop with 20 portcos — outreach, sponsor verification, comps research, valuation review, commentary drafting, red-flag cross-check. Built on real Anthropic API, six of seven agents adapt patterns from Anthropic's open-source financial-services repo.

Why I'm writing: {specific reason tied to their firm — e.g. "your fund just closed Fund III at $2B; the quarterly close on a fresh portfolio is the moment to set the cycle up right"}.

Not pitching anything. Would value 15 min to hear how you're approaching AI in your portfolio ops. Free this {Thursday or Friday}?
```

**Why this works:** leads with proof (the Farol work), names the specific signal, ends with a low-friction ask. The "not pitching anything" line is true because the first call isn't a pitch — but it reduces the prospect's guard.

## Day 5 — Cold email #1

Subject lines to A/B (pick one per batch of 10):
- `quick note on your Q{N} cycle`
- `seven Claude agents, twenty portcos`
- `your {specific event} + AI for the close`
- `is anyone at {Firm name} running the quarterly cycle in Claude?`

Body:

```
{First name},

I run Bridgeworks, a small AI firm that builds quarterly-cycle agents for PE shops. We just shipped a 7-agent system for a fund with 20 portcos — outreach drafter, sponsor data verifier, comps researcher, valuation reviewer, commentary drafter, red-flag cross-check, intelligence monitor. All on the real Anthropic API. Six adapt patterns from Anthropic's open-source financial-services repo.

Why I'm writing: {specific signal — e.g. "you closed Fund IV last month; the next quarterly cycle is the cleanest time to wire AI into the process"}.

Two questions:
1. Who owns quarterly process at {Firm}? If it's not you, who should I reach out to?
2. Open to a 15-min call this {week or next}? Not selling anything — comparing notes on AI for PE ops.

Best,
Damian
{LinkedIn URL}
{Phone, optional}
```

**Why this works:** opens with proof, names a real signal, asks two short questions (one is easy to answer even if the other is no), closes without a calendar link.

**Why this works:** delivers value with no ask. The case study URL is the proof. The "door's open whenever" line is patient — it works on the ~6-12 month timeline that complex PE buying actually moves on.

## Day 21 — Breakup email

Subject: `last note from Bridgeworks`

```
{First name},

Going to stop reaching out — clearly not the right timing for {Firm}.

If anything changes — new portfolio company, new quarter approaching, new ops head joining — I'm one reply away. Have a good {Q3 or whatever quarter we're in}.

Best,
Damian
```

**Why this works:** breakup emails consistently get the highest reply rate of any single touch in B2B sequences (sometimes 5–10% on their own). Removing urgency makes it psychologically easier to reply.

## When someone replies positively

Within 4 hours, send:

```
{First name},

Great — let me know what works.

Slots open:
- {Thursday}: 11am ET, 2pm ET, 4pm ET
- {Friday}: 10am ET, 1pm ET

Or send a couple of times that work for you and I'll grab one. We can do Zoom or Google Meet, you pick.

Best,
Damian
```

**Why this works:** specific times reduce back-and-forth. Their pick. Manual scheduling, not a Calendly link (feels less transactional at this price point).

## When someone replies "send more info"

Avoid the trap of writing a 600-word email summary. Send:

```
{First name},

Two short links beat any pitch I could write:

1. What we build, who it's for: bridgeworks.co
2. The 7-agent diligence case: bridgeworks.co/work/farol.html

Happy to set up a 15-min call to walk you through either. {Tuesday or Wednesday} next week work?

Best,
Damian
```

**Why this works:** lets the prospect self-qualify on the site, keeps the ask alive. Doesn't dump everything into an email they won't read.

## When someone replies "we already do this internally"

```
{First name},

That's the right answer for most firms doing this seriously. The work we typically add: the part that hurts most weeks, that internal teams keep deprioritizing because the deal team needs them on something more visible.

In one fund we work with, it's the column-reference rolling in the investor report. In another, it's the comparable-company refresh. In a third, it's the LP commentary blocks. Always the same shape: high-volume, low-judgment, error-prone, repeated quarterly.

If anything like that exists at {Firm}, 15 min to compare notes might be useful. If not, totally understand.

Best,
Damian
```

**Why this works:** doesn't argue with their internal team. Names three specific high-pain points that exist at almost every PE firm. Opens the door without insisting.

```
{First name},

Great to connect, and thanks {Introducer name} for the intro.

Quick context: I run a 2-person AI firm called Bridgeworks. We build quarterly-cycle agents for PE shops — recent work is a 7-agent system handling outreach, sponsor verification, comps, valuation review, commentary, and red-flag review across 20 portcos.

I'd value 15–20 min to hear how you're thinking about AI on the ops side. Free {two specific time slots}? Or send what works for you.

Best,
Damian
```

**Why this works:** acknowledges the introducer (closes the loop they care about). Compact proof. Direct ask with two specific slots.
